Business & Media

Partnerships with a reason to belong here.

BlakeThomasPierce.com is the independent home of author, musician and storyteller Blake Thomas Pierce. Partnership opportunities are considered when there is a natural connection to readers and the subjects explored across the site.

Good partnership fits

Relevant categories may include books and reading, writing and publishing tools, music, family and heritage, American and working-class culture, practical lifestyle products, and services genuinely useful to the audience.

The goal is not to fill pages with unrelated promotions. A partnership should make sense beside the stories readers came here to find.

Available opportunities

Potential collaborations can include clearly labeled site sponsorships, sponsored editorial projects where appropriate, book or music-related campaigns, interviews, media appearances, giveaways and longer-term brand relationships.

Availability, placement and terms are considered individually. Audience trust and editorial independence come first.

Disclosure standard

Paid relationships will be identified clearly. Sponsorship does not purchase a favorable review or opinion. Advertising and sponsored material will be distinguishable from independent editorial content.

Business inquiries

For sponsorship, media, publishing or collaboration inquiries, contact Blake through the official author Facebook presence while dedicated business contact infrastructure is expanded.
