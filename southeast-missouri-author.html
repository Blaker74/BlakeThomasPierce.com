A story rooted close to home

Three-Quarters Green is Blake Thomas Pierce's novel about Lake Farmer, a boy growing up in humble circumstances and carrying the people, work, humor, losses and ordinary memories of that childhood into adulthood.

The story draws its emotional landscape from the kind of rural and working-class life familiar across Southeast Missouri: trailers, old cars, long workdays, family sayings, making do with what you have and realizing years later that ordinary moments were never ordinary at all.

For Southeast Missouri readers: this is not a story looking down on working people from the outside. It is a story written from inside that world, with affection for the people whose lives may never make headlines but still matter.

About Blake

Blake Thomas Pierce is an independent author, musician and storyteller from Southeast Missouri. Before publishing fiction, he spent decades in the heavy-duty truck industry, building relationships with customers and solving real-world problems. His writing focuses on family, memory, grief, humor, perseverance and working people whose stories deserve to be remembered.
