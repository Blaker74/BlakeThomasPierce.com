Three-Quarters Green

Three-Quarters Green follows Lake Farmer through a life built from humble beginnings, hard work, love, fatherhood, change and loss. The story looks closely at the memories people often overlook until years later, when those ordinary details become the landmarks of an entire life.

It is a story for readers who know that a childhood home does not have to be grand to become unforgettable, and that the people who shape us often do it through small acts that only make sense in hindsight.

The Words Between Us

Set between remote Alaska and Seattle, The Words Between Us centers on Raven, a gifted young inventor whose mother suffers a stroke that leaves her cognitively aware but unable to form understandable words.

Raven's determination to reach her becomes the driving force behind NOVA—Neural Output Vocal Assistance—a technology designed to translate neural signals into audible language. At its heart, the novel is not about technology. It is about the distance between two people when love remains intact but words disappear, and the lengths a son will go to cross that distance.

About the stories

The books share a common concern even when their settings and plots are very different: people matter. Families are complicated. Work leaves marks on us. Loss changes the meaning of old memories. Hope can survive in places where it would be easier to give up.
