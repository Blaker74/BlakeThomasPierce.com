AN HONEST LOOK AT THE MUSIC

Human stories.
AI-assisted music.

I’m Blake Pierce, and I want you to know how my music is made. I write the lyrics and use AI tools to help create the vocals and instrumental arrangements.

Every song takes work: writing and rewriting, shaping the composition, choosing the musical direction, and searching for the instruments, voice, rhythm, and melody that bring the words to life. I listen, revise, and keep refining until the sound carries the feeling behind the song.

The stories come from a human life: family, loss, love, struggle, and the hope that keeps us going. My goal is to turn those experiences into carefully crafted music with the warmth, depth, and emotional connection I look for in any song.

AI helps me create the soundtrack.
The life behind it is mine.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORDS

From here.
From the heart.

I’m a husband, dad, granddad, and working man from Southeast Missouri. Writing has always given me somewhere to put the things I feel.

I grew up loving words. My daddy couldn’t read or write, and that has made my own relationship with them deeply personal. Music gives those words another way to reach someone.

Some songs carry grief. Some carry a grin. All of them begin with something I want to say.

— Blake
