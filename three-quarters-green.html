A Novel by Blake Pierce

Three-Quarters Green

A story about where we come from, who walks beside us and the moments we do not realize are precious until years later.

A life built one ordinary day at a time

Three-Quarters Green follows Lake Farmer through a childhood shaped by humble circumstances, early work, family, friendship and the kind of resourcefulness learned when there is no easy way around a problem.

As Lake grows older, the scenery changes—jobs, marriage, children, moves, losses and new beginnings—but the heart of the story remains close to home. It is about the people who form us and the memories that gain weight as the years pass.

Why the story matters: Not every meaningful life looks remarkable from the outside. This book treats working people, imperfect families, hard seasons, laughter and perseverance as things worth remembering.

For readers who recognize the details

The story is rooted in a working-class American world of trailers, old cars, long days, family sayings, first jobs and people figuring things out with what they have. Those details are not scenery added afterward. They are the landscape of the story.

At its center is the idea that a life does not have to be famous to matter. The smallest memories can become the ones we would give anything to experience once more.

About Blake Pierce

Blake Thomas Pierce is an American author, musician and storyteller from Southeast Missouri. His writing draws from family, working-class experience, grief, humor, faith, perseverance and the overlooked details of everyday life.
