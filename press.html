Blake Thomas Pierce

Blake Thomas Pierce is an American independent author, musician and storyteller from Southeast Missouri whose work focuses on family, working-class experience, memory, loss, perseverance and hope.

Short biography

After decades working in and around the heavy-duty truck industry, Blake turned a lifetime of observed details, family stories and lived experience into fiction and music. His storytelling favors recognizable people, emotional honesty and direct language over polished perfection.

Books

Three-Quarters Green is a character-driven story following Lake Farmer through childhood, work, love, fatherhood and loss, with an emphasis on the ordinary memories that eventually become the landmarks of a life.

Three-Quarters Green

The Words Between Us follows Raven, a gifted young inventor whose mother's stroke leads him toward the creation of NOVA, a fictional neural communication technology. Beneath the technology is a family story about love when spoken language disappears.

The Words Between Us
