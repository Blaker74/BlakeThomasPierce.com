Official Press & Media Information

Blake Pierce · BTP Records

Blake Pierce is an independent country artist, songwriter and storyteller from Southeast Missouri. Releasing through BTP Records, his album Spotify’s My Diary brings together 15 songs about family, working life, home, loss and perseverance.

Short biography

After decades working in and around the heavy-duty truck industry, Blake turned a lifetime of observed details, family stories and lived experience into fiction and music. His storytelling favors recognizable people, emotional honesty and direct language over polished perfection.
