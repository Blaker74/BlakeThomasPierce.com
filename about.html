About the Author

Blake Thomas Pierce

Blake Thomas Pierce is an American author, musician and storyteller from Southeast Missouri. His writing is shaped by working-class life, family, faith, grief, perseverance and the belief that ordinary people carry stories worth preserving.

Long before publishing fiction, Blake spent decades in the heavy-duty truck industry. It is a world built around practical problems, long days, relationships and the expectation that when somebody needs an answer, you find one. That background helped shape a direct approach to storytelling: recognizable people, concrete details and respect for lives that are rarely treated as glamorous.

Why he writes

Blake's work is interested in memory—especially the way small things become important only after time has passed. A home, a car, a job, a family phrase, a meal, a long drive or a person who is no longer there can eventually carry the weight of an entire period of life.

His first published novel, Three-Quarters Green, grew from that perspective. His next novel, The Words Between Us, takes the same interest in human connection into a very different story about a young inventor, his mother and the possibility of recovering communication after devastating loss.

Books, music and storytelling

Writing is only one part of Blake's creative work. He has also released original music, including “Jesse and You” and “Drink This Sunday Away.” Whether the form is a novel or a song, the goal is similar: tell the truth of a feeling clearly enough that somebody else recognizes it.
