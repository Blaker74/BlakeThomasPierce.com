Behind the Music

Blake Pierce

Blake Pierce is an independent country artist and songwriter from Southeast Missouri, releasing music through BTP Records. His songs draw on working-class life, family, love, loss and the places he calls home.

Before sharing his stories through songs and fiction, Blake spent decades in the heavy-duty truck industry. It is a world built around practical problems, long days, relationships and the expectation that when somebody needs an answer, you find one. That background helped shape a direct approach to storytelling: recognizable people, concrete details and respect for lives that are rarely treated as glamorous.

Why he writes

Blake's work is interested in memory—especially the way small things become important only after time has passed. A home, a car, a job, a family phrase, a meal, a long drive or a person who is no longer there can eventually carry the weight of an entire period of life.

His first published novel, Three-Quarters Green, grew from that perspective.

Music, books and storytelling

Music is at the center of Blake’s creative work. His original songs are inspired by family, home and working life, with the novel Three-Quarters Green offering another way into those stories. Whether the form is a novel or a song, the goal is similar: tell the truth of a feeling clearly enough that somebody else recognizes it.
